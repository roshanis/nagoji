The book runs on to the Mannar truce (ch 18) and the Thrippadidanam of 1750 (ch 28, "The monsoon of 1750... Padmanabha Dasa"). The VOC was a chartered company, not a state, so "European company" (or "the Dutch East India Company") follows the Note. The pack wisely avoids "first"; keep it that way.
