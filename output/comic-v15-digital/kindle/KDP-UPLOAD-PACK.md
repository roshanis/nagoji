### Description

> In a Portuguese cellar under Goa, the first thing they take is his name. A shrug, and an entry in a book.
>
> Nagoji Sawant rode with the Maratha household horse. Captured by the Portuguese, he is shipped south in chains. A storm tears the ship apart. He lives.
>
> He washes up on the pepper coast of Kerala, where every kingdom is a bargain and every bargain is backed by steel. In Travancore, the ambitious king Marthanda Varma is breaking the old noble houses and forging a single state. The Dutch East India Company wants that state divided.

> Pulled into court as a useful stranger, Nagoji is set to train horsemen for a war no one can afford to lose. ## Before you press Publish

- Make the GitHub repository roshanis/nagoji private, or remove the comic PDFs from it. It is public and holds earlier versions of these pages (V14 chapters 6 to 17, the V13 reference PDF). KDP can hold a book whose pages it finds free online until you prove your rights, and KDP Select does not allow it. The same goes for the web edition if it is hosted anywhere public.
- Cover: A (chosen, and already inside the EPUB).
- Confirm the copyright and credit text on the copyright page.
- Check the book in Kindle Previewer 3 on your Mac; the online previewer may stall on a 311 MB file.
- Once published, the book goes live in about 72 hours. A new file can be uploaded later for the 2e changes and the exact panel zoom. Readers who already own the book get the update only if you ask KDP support to send it.
- The novel already sells on Kindle under the same title and author. Once the comic is live, check that Amazon has not shown it as another edition on the novel's page. If it has, ask KDP support to separate them.
- 72 hours is the usual review time, but a large comic that declares AI content can be held for manual review. Submit as soon as you can.
