Wide, full page width. The mandapa of Padmanabhaswamy at night. Oil lamps in brass holders burn along every wall and pillar, their flames doubled in the polished stone floor until the whole hall seems to float on fire. Carved granite pillars recede into shadow. Beyond an open outer doorway, monsoon rain falls in a silver sheet. The nobles of Travancore stand packed in ranks, bare-chested in temple fashion, fine mundus and shoulder cloths, gold at their ears and throats catching the lamplight.
