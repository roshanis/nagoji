REVIEW CORRECTION (an earlier image of this panel was rejected): Keep the composition: the wide view from behind and beside the gun on the rocky ridge, the bright muzzle flame, the great bank of white smoke rolling out over the thorn valley, the burst of dark birds exploding out of the trees across the valley, and the open late-afternoon sky above. Change the gun crew to match the previous panel: sweating bare-chested men in white mundu with plain white cotton head cloths, no waistcoats, no long shirts, no turbans. Put no red, rust, orange or maroon turban, sash or cloth on anyone. A rust-red turban with a red sash is Nagoji's old commander look, and red cloth belongs to Ponnan alone in this chapter. The gun is a plain muzzle-loading field gun on a two-wheeled wooden carriage. The gunner stands clear to one side of the carriage and touches a linstock to the touch-hole at arm's length, not leaning over the barrel. The white draught oxen stay back behind the gun.

SETTING (this panel): A rocky ridge crest above a shallow valley choked with low thorn bushes and outcrops, where the riders rein in and the light gun on its two-wheeled bullock carriage is hauled to the edge. The valley floor lies far below between the thorn stands. Light: late afternoon. No wall, fort or village as the gun's target; no European gunners, uniforms or coats; no blood, and any stains read dark, never red.
