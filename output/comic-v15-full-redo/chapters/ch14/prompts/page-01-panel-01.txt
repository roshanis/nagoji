Wide, full page width. A weary Travancore column in monsoon-wet paddy, turning north. Bullocks and men haul cannon through ankle-deep mud along a narrow village bund. Mid-column, Nagoji on Kayal, his bay mare with a coat the ochre red-brown of earth after rain. Nagoji in the commander look: rust-red turban, cream tunic, broad red sash, cream trousers, boots, talwar; thick curled moustache, clean-shaven chin, small gold ear stud; no stubble. Mud to the mare's knees. Grey sky.
