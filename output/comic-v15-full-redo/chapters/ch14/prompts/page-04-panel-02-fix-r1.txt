The Dutch camp seen over our parapet. Mud ramparts sagging under their own guns, one cannon tilted into slumped earth. Sick men lying under torn canvas. Empty barrels stacked by a wall. Among the sick, small in frame, Lieutenant Rijtel: a short, dark-haired Dutch officer in a blue coat with an officer's sash.
