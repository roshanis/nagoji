V15 graphic novel panel page-09-panel-01 for chapter 14. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Colachel beach below the burned stockade.

Wide. Sunset in lurid purples and reds through the smoke. The stockade burned down to glowing embers. The Dutch survivors huddled on the beach, some kneeling, some standing hollow-eyed with hands raised, their blue coats stained with ash.

PEOPLE IN THIS PANEL: no principal characters.

ART RULES:
- 1740s dress, arms, objects.
- No blood or gore; stage aftermath.
- Clay or brass oil lamps; open wicks.
- Non-Maratha raiders' and rivals' banners are plain cloth.
- Dutch East India Company soldiers wear blue coats. No soldier, Indian or European, wears a British red coat.

LETTERING: the letterer adds it afterwards, so draw none of it. This panel will carry exactly 1 lettering area(s): a caption (short). Leave clear, low-detail space (sky, wall, floor or shadow) for them, preferably in the upper part of the frame. Keep faces, hands and important action outside that space. Draw NO balloons, caption boxes, panel borders or a border line around the picture, and no written characters, numerals, pseudo-writing, logos or watermark anywhere, including on banners, flags, cloth, walls and documents. Any balloon, caption or letterer wording in the description means space only, never painted lettering.

SETTING (this panel): The wide Colachel beach below the burned-down Dutch stockade, its timber palisade reduced to glowing embers and smoke drifting low over the sand. Dutch survivors in ash-stained blue coats huddle on the sand under guard of Nair spearmen in white mundus, and when the sea is in view the Dutch ships are only small, distant shapes. Light: sunset in lurid purples and reds through smoke. No blood or bodies, no ropes at any prisoner's neck, no turban on any Nair, no British red coats, no lettering on flags.
