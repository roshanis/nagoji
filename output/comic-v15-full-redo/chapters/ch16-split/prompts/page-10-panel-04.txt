The ride back through green country. Varma on horseback, still in the plain white cloth, no crest, no jewels, the sword laid across his knees as if it were a living thing. Nagoji riding at his side on Kayal, his bay mare with the ochre red-brown coat, watching him (commander dress, clean chin, no grey yet).
