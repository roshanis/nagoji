V15 graphic novel panel page-03-panel-04 for chapter 27. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Siege lines below the Kottarakkara hill town.

Full width, bottom. Dusk, months on, outside the besieged hill town. A road blocked with felled palms. A canal dammed with packed earth. Merchant carts turned back by Travancore sentries (Nair men in white mundu with spears and matchlocks; NOT blue coats, NOT red coats) while supply carts roll the other way toward the siege lines. On a bund, two officers watch: Eustachius De Lannoy (a head taller, pale hair going grey, clean-shaven European face, plain white Travancore coat with no red waistcoat; NOT a Dutch blue coat) and Nagoji (a head shorter, dark topknot, grey at the temples, thick moustache, clean-shaven chin, red waistcoat under a white coat, silver chain). On the hill beyond, the old palace, ringed by the first siege fires.
