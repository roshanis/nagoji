V15 graphic novel panel page-09-panel-05 for chapter 27. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Path west through the palms.

Full width, bottom. Evening. Nair guards (white mundu, spears) lead Savitri, a small white figure with bound hands, away down a path west through the palms. At the very edge of the horizon, a pale band of sea haze. Smoke from the burned fields drifts across the sky and mixes with the haze. Nagoji in the foreground, seen from behind (dark topknot with grey at the temples, white coat; no turban), watching. Compose it to echo 1.5: the same rising smoke, a different fire.

FRAME SHAPE: a wide horizontal strip, about 3 to 1 (width to height), filling the whole width; keep the key action inside the middle half of the height.
