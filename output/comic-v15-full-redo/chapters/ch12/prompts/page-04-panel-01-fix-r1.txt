Wide action. Near Nagercoil, where red-earth scrub and palmyra palms break into stream-cut, rocky ground at the foot of the hills. Nagoji, mounted, holds the mouth of a ravine with Dhanaji at his shoulder and a line of Nair musketmen kneeling among the rocks, matchlocks levelled; the Nairs are bare-headed with a front topknot, white mundu hitched for fighting. Dhanaji: stockier and broader than Nagoji, short trimmed beard, white Maratha pagdi turban, indigo quilted tunic, no red sash, talwar drawn. Only Nagoji wears the rust-red turban and red sash. Out on the open scrub beyond, Chanda Sahib's riders in dust-coloured quilted coats and dark indigo or black turbans, no green, no red sashes, wheel away from the ravine mouth, faster, refusing the broken ground. Dust. No one falls.
