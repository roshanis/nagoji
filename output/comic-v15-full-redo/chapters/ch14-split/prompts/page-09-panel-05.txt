V15 graphic novel panel page-09-panel-05 for chapter 14. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Open sand between the trenches and the burning stockade.

Full width, bottom. Nagoji's view from a slight rise at the palm line, looking down and across at the Dutch square through thinning smoke. Its front ranks are packed tight against the Nair line; its rear ranks are packed and facing the burning fort. Between them, the flank that faces the scrubland has thinned to a single ragged file with daylight showing through the gaps, and an officer's arm drags men out of that file toward the front.
