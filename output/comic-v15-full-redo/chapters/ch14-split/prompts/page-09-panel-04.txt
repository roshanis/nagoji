The left flank, a grove of palms in deep shade. Nagoji on Kayal, the taller bay mare, among Maravar, Madurai and Deccan riders. Nagoji in the commander look: rust-red turban, cream tunic, broad red sash, cream trousers, boots, talwar; thick curled moustache, clean-shaven chin, small gold ear stud; no stubble. Dhanaji (white turban, indigo-dyed tunic) mounted at his shoulder. Ponnan beside them: bare-headed, curly hair uncovered, red shoulder cloth, white mundu hitched above bare knees, short jacket, on a smaller, lighter Maravar horse, a head lower than Nagoji. Nagoji leaning forward in the saddle, staring through the smoke.
