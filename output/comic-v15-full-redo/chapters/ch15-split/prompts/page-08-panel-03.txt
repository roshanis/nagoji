Wide, pure speed. The gallop south along the beach track, coconut palms blurred into a green wall. Nagoji leading on Kayal (bay mare, ochre red-brown coat), rust-red turban and red sash, jaw clenched, one hand pressed for a moment to his bruised ribs. Dhanaji beside him on his brown horse, white turban, indigo tunic. Maravar scouts and Madurai lancers strung out behind.

PEOPLE IN THIS PANEL: Dhanaji, Nagoji. Draw each named person once, with no doubles.
