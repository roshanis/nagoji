Wide, low angle up the rise where the main battery stood. Marthanda Varma (varma-v1 face; cream and gold, peacock crest, gold necklaces) waiting there, spear upright. Below him Nair infantry line both sides of the Dutch path, silent and impassive: bare-chested, forward-tied topknots, short white mundus, small round shields, spears. No coats, no uniforms, no hats. At Varma's side a Nair holds a white conch shell at his hip, not yet raised.
