V15 graphic novel panel page-04-panel-01 for chapter 14. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Colachel surf at night.

Wide. The black surf, and on it, shadows. Low, narrow boats blackened with soot slip through the waves. Mukkuvar men, bodies oiled and glistening, paddle almost without a splash.

PEOPLE IN THIS PANEL: no principal characters.

ART RULES:
- 1740s dress, arms, objects.
- No blood or gore; stage aftermath.
- Clay or brass oil lamps; open wicks.

LETTERING: the letterer adds it afterwards, so draw none of it. This panel will carry exactly 1 lettering area(s): a caption (short). Leave clear, low-detail space (sky, wall, floor or shadow) for them, preferably in the upper part of the frame. Keep faces, hands and important action outside that space. Draw NO balloons, caption boxes, panel borders or a border line around the picture, and no written characters, numerals, pseudo-writing, logos or watermark anywhere, including on banners, flags, cloth, walls and documents. Any balloon, caption or letterer wording in the description means space only, never painted lettering.

SETTING (this panel): The open Colachel beach at night, far from the batteries: wet black sand, a heaving black surf and monsoon cloud over a hidden moon. The Mukkuvar fishermen's low, narrow plank boats are blackened with soot, and the men are oiled and bare-chested in loincloths. Light: night, faint moonlight through monsoon cloud. No ships, lanterns or lights on the water; no batteries, tents or stockade in view; no glass lantern on the shore.
