V15 graphic novel panel page-26-panel-01 for chapter 24. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Drill ground below the new bastion.

Wide. The drill ground. In the foreground, a Nair captain (older, heavier, ash stripes on his forehead) muttering to Nagoji (in uniform, silver chain over the coat, no forehead mark). Behind them, the Prince in the same white coat as the others, visibly the youngest and slightest man on the ground, beardless and narrow-shouldered, hauling a collapsed recruit into the shade and holding his own water skin to the man's lips.
