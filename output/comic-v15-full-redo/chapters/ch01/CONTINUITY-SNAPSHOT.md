# V15 continuity bible

Authoritative for every script and every generated frame. Where it disagrees with an
earlier script (including CHAPTER-01-SCRIPT.md's "short dark beard"), THIS FILE WINS.

Visual identity comes from the user-approved V13 lock:
`output/comic-v13-reference-redesign-v1/CHARACTER-LOCK-v1.json` and its concept sheets
(`concepts/nagoji-v2.png`, `concepts/varma-v1.png`, `concepts/temple-priest-v1.png`).
Attach the relevant sheet as a reference image to EVERY generation that shows that character.

## Nagoji Sawant / Ananthan Pillai

Constant identity: strong cheekbones, straight nose, thick curled moustache, CLEAN-SHAVEN
CHIN (light stubble at most, captivity only), long curly black hair, small gold ear stud,
lean cavalryman build. No forehead marks, no sacred thread, no royal jewellery. No facial scar.

## Other principals

- **Marthanda Varma**: V13 varma-v1 face. Court: cream and gold, peacock crest, gold necklaces, often a spear, frequently bare-chested. Ch28 dedication: same face aged, low knot, plain mundu, bare torso, no jewels, no headwear.
- **Ramayyan Dalawa**: thin, sharp fine features, receding hair, plain white cloth, palm-leaf bundles and stylus. Ch14 battle: mud-splattered white dhoti, curved Nair sword.
- **Padmini Amma**: in her fifties at first; thick black hair coiled at the nape, going grey later; simple gold; cotton sari hitched for walking; long walking stick. Dies ch27.
- **Revathi Bayi**: a little younger than Nagoji; deep blue or indigo sari with gold, deep green in ch11; jasmine in a braid; flat-coin necklace; gold armlets; sandalwood line at the hairline. Widow until ch20, then wears a tali. Grey threading from ch20.
- **Father Duarte**: thin Portuguese Jesuit, scholar's stoop, grey tired eyes, ink-stained fingers, callused right hand, plain black cassock with a small cross. NO white Roman collar tab (a 19th-century anachronism). Ch1-2; returns older and greyer in ch24-25 and white-haired in ch28.
- **Joao**: broad, heavy, ruddy Portuguese gaoler; brown leather jerkin over a cream shirt; wide belt; iron key ring. NOT a priest, no cassock, no collar. Ch1-2 only.
- **Keshavrao**: young, narrow shoulders, black hair hacked short, prisoner rags. Ch2-3.
- **Ibrahim Marakkar** ("kapitan"): shorter than the fishermen but broader, scar from the LEFT ear to the jaw, a coastal Muslim broker. Short coat over a vest with bulging pockets from ch6.
- **Eustachius De Lannoy**: tall, pale hair. A distant figure in a Dutch blue coat in ch11; torn and soot-stained in ch14-15; white mundu and cotton coat from ch15; white Travancore coat in ch24; grey by ch28.
- **Raza Khan**: lean, hawk nose, pointed moustache, scar on his ANATOMICAL LEFT cheek, green high-wrapped turban. Madurai lancer from ch7.
- **Ponnan (Ponnam Pandya Deven)**: broad-shouldered Maravar, curly hair, thick moustache, clean chin, red shoulder cloth, white mundu hitched, short jacket.
- **Dhanaji**: a Deccan rider of Nagoji's generation, talwar. Leaves for the Deccan in ch20.
- **Karl August**: a pale German deserter with a sickly yellow cast, cropped greying hair, rough cotton shirt. Ch11.
- **Mathoo Tharakan**: an older Syrian Christian merchant with grey receding hair, a fine grey moustache, a gold cross and white cloth. Heavy gold chains and a silk tunic from ch15.
- **The Senior Rani of Attingal**: Varma's sister; dressed in white; cool and measured.
- **The Prince / heir**: about ten in ch17 and ch22-23, about fourteen in ch24.
- **Savitri of Kottarakkara**: older, with lines of care; white mourning clothes in ch27.
- **Thoma Ittyerah** (ch24): a Syrian Christian artillery commander, broad, in a white tunic, with betel-stained teeth.
- **Avraham ben Ephraim** (ch24): a Kochi Jewish trader in a dark coat and cap, with a chest-length beard.
- **Nandini** (ch27): a small girl, Padmini's sister's daughter and heir.
- **Temple priest face** (V13): ONLY for a confirmed Hindu ritual priest. Never for Duarte, Ramayyan or bystanders.

## Standing rules for generated art

- No text of any kind in the art: blank balloon and caption reserves only, lettering composited later.
- No nooses. Interrogation ropes hang from an iron ring for binding wrists, never tied as hanging nooses.
- Portuguese guards must not read as British redcoats or as Dutch blue-coats. The Dutch VOC soldiers are the blue coats.
- No blood or gore. Violence is shown through staging, distance and aftermath.
- Period: 1738-1753 Malabar coast, Travancore and Portuguese Goa. No anachronisms.
- Print: every placed image must reach at least 300 effective PPI at its printed size. Below that, upscale 2x with `~/AI/upscalers/upscale2x.py` (Real-ESRGAN x2plus, BSD-3-Clause) before placement, or regenerate.
