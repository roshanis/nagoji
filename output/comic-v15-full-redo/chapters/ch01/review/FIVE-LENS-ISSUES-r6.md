# Chapter 1 r6: issues confirmed by the five-lens review (2026-09-30)

Source: FIVE-LENS-REVIEW-r6-2026-09-30.json (5 reviewers, each serious finding checked by a skeptic; 33 of 36 confirmed, 45 minor notes). Duplicates across lenses are merged below. Panel numbers follow the script.

## Identity and anachronism
1. 6.4: Duarte wears the white Roman collar tab the bible forbids.
2. 7.5: Duarte's close-up (the mouth-twitch beat the script says pays off in chapter 24) is off-model: broad jowls, white hair, not the gaunt face of his sheet.
3. 8.3: the priest in the overhead wide is not Duarte (dark balding hair, moustache, white collar edge).
4. 8.5: Duarte's pectoral is a large ornate crucifix; everywhere else it is a small plain cross.
5. 2.3 and 3.4: Joao loses his full beard (moustache or goatee only), so he reads as a different gaoler.
6. 6.5: the pre-capture memory of Nagoji with Kanka shows a bearded man with a thin moustache in a grey blanket, not Nagoji.
7. 4.1: the escorting guards wear 16th-century morion and cabasset helmets with red jerkins; they contradict the tricorne guards of 9.2 and read as a century too early.

## Nagoji's restraints (chapter 1 row: ankle irons only, bandaged hands)
8. 2.2: an iron wrist manacle with a wall chain over his shoulder.
9. 4.2: wrists chained together (contradicts 4.1 and 4.4).
10. 4.3 and 4.4: only one ankle ironed.
11. 8.6: dark wrist manacles, bandages gone.
12. 4.4: a maroon sash (minor).

## Setting and story
13. Pages 5 to 8: the interrogation chamber changes from panel to panel (sunlit rib vault with an arched window, a chapel with altar crosses, a pillared hall), so the scene no longer reads as one sound-swallowing dungeon room.
14. 4.4: the walk ends with Nagoji alone at the doorway; Duarte and Joao, who escort him in 4.2, have vanished.
15. 9.1: the aftermath of torture is lost; Nagoji sits relaxed in clean, neat bandages.
16. 6.4: Joao is large in the foreground looking up, where the script puts him in the background not even looking up (minor).

## Lettering and layout
17. 7.5: Joao's off-panel balloon runs off the right frame with its tail cut.
18. 8.6: Duarte's two lines sit by Joao at the right; long tails cross other figures to reach him.
19. Page 6: panels 6.2 to 6.4 are inset to different widths with uneven gutters (minor).
