The order to fall out. Coats shrugged open, hats pushed back, dhotis loosened, men grinning up at Nagoji in his foreign cloth.

Nagoji: Constant identity: strong cheekbones, straight nose, thick curled moustache, CLEAN-SHAVEN CHIN, long curly black hair, small gold ear stud, lean cavalryman build. No forehead marks, no sacred thread, no royal jewellery. No facial scar.; Commander sheet: rust-red turban, cream tunic, broad red sash, cream trousers, boots, talwar. Nails healed but ridged.
