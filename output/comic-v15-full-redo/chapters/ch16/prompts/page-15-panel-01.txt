SETTING (this panel): A long flat Travancore drill ground of beaten earth and pale sand below laterite and granite walls, palms along its edge, a low wall and a small timber dais at one side, and a thin band of sea on the horizon. Firing lines are seen side-on, and anyone watching stands behind or beside the line, never in front of the muzzles. Light: daylight under a thin warm wash that fades toward the full palette. No ship close inshore, any hull only a tiny shape at anchor on the horizon; no red coats, blue coats only where the panel names them and never on De Lannoy, and no chairs.
