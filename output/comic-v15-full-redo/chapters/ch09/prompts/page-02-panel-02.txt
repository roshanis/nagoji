Wide, inside the ring. The ground rises toward a central hall with a tiled roof, banners hanging heavy and still. Women along the verandahs in plain cotton and bright silk, heads uncovered, hair oiled and coiled or braided. Fewer men, in the same Nair dress as the guards: an older, heavy-set clerk with a palm-leaf bundle and an iron stylus (NOT Ramayyan: round face, greying moustache), and one with a sword, both glancing at a doorway where no one has yet appeared. Padmini and Nagoji dismounting in the foreground, Padmini drawing her long walking stick from the saddle.
