V15 graphic novel panel page-15-panel-01 for chapter 20. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Velinadu courtyard just after the tali is tied.

Wide. The drums louder, triumphant. Women ululating. Dhanaji (white turban, indigo tunic) whooping with a fist in the air like a boy at a horse fair. At the centre the couple sit still, looking only at each other, the *tali* at her throat.

PEOPLE IN THIS PANEL: Dhanaji, Nagoji, Revathi Bayi. Draw each named person once, with no doubles.

LOOKS (face, build, hair, facial hair, scars, marks and ear ornament always follow these lines; footwear, sleeves, headwear, props in hand and pose follow the panel description above wherever it is more specific):
Dhanaji: a Deccan rider of Nagoji's generation, stockier and heavier than Nagoji, short black beard, white Maratha pagdi turban, indigo quilted tunic, no sash, talwar. Never a rust-red turban.
Nagoji: Constant identity: strong cheekbones, straight nose, a THICK, bushy curled handlebar moustache, CLEAN-SHAVEN CHIN, thick curly black hair, one tiny flat gold stud flush on the earlobe with nothing hanging from it, lean cavalryman build. No forehead marks, no sacred thread, no royal jewellery, no pearls. No facial scar.; The same, with no knife at his waist (he gives it to Dhanaji in 10.3 to 10.5).
Revathi Bayi: a little younger than Nagoji; deep blue or indigo sari with gold; jasmine in a braid; flat-coin necklace; gold armlets; sandalwood line at the hairline. Wears a tali; grey threading in her hair.
