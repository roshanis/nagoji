V15 graphic novel panel page-04-panel-03 for chapter 10. Drawn in the book's inked graphic-novel style: clean ink line and painted colour, never photorealistic. Place: Travancore fort drill ground.

The drill master at the near flank of the line, well clear of the muzzles, arm flung down, mouth open on the word of command. Ramayyan and Nagoji at the left edge, behind the line.
