> CAPTION: Velinadu Kovilakam was not the city my Deccan mind had expected.

## PAGE 6

Five panels. Her line's history, the brand, the reason. The first thing they agree on.

**6.2** Insert, tight. Nagoji's left forearm as he pushes the cream tunic sleeve up past the elbow. **6.3** Revathi holding a piece of plantain between finger and thumb, not eating it. No bleed here: hold on her and the plantain.

> NAGOJI (off): The ground does not care whose blood it drinks.

**6.5** Revathi's eyes narrowing slightly. She eats the plantain at last, slowly, as if chewing helps her think.

## Adaptation notes

- **Page count.** 12 pages against a target of 10 (range 8 to 12); page 4 was split in two on 2026-10-04 so that its lettering fits. The chapter is 3,062 words and almost all of it is one conversation, so the pages go to Revathi (pages 3 to 10) and to the night verandah (page 12), which plants the relationship that pays off in chapter 20. The ride and arrival are compressed to two pages.
- **Ride compressed.** The ponds, shrines, fields, bullocks and children are folded into one landscape panel (1.2). The first caption gives the purpose of the ride, which the art cannot show, and "weighed" sets up Padmini's "That is part of the weighing" (2.4). Padmini's muted silk and sword are kept; the bible's walking stick is strapped to the saddle on page 1 and in her hand from the dismount onward.
- **Cut at the gate.** Padmini's "tongues still loose from sleep" line, and the guards "more focused on who entered than what", which 2.1 plays in the art.
- **Cut from the conversation.** Revathi's list of the king's deeds (Padmanabhapuram, forts, Dutchmen and Marakkars); "Now everyone speaks of the Maharaja's reforms"; Ramayyan's "grove of palm leaves"; "There was nothing easy about it. We lost men every time. Horses also"; the "flag over every fort from Nashik to this coast" line; the "Land of queens" exchange; "He knows that if he wants a strong kingdom..."; the Kottarakkara chief's grandmothers; the monsoon-humidity line (7.5 now plays that silence without words); the second half of "I stayed because he fights Europeans"; "Ramayyan marks your uses", "a general of horse" and "A place in their tales"; and the dowries-and-boats clause of Revathi's invitation. Her long speeches are trimmed to their strongest sentences, which stay verbatim.
- **Cut from the close.** "Revathi Bayi had made her position clear", since 10.1 to 10.5 already make it clear, and "For now, I followed Padmini into the hall", which 12.5 shows. The "remained unanswered" line becomes a question caption, and the chapter ends on the novel's own closing image.
- **Kept verbatim** for later payoff: "Not from mine" (chapter 20, when she takes the tali), the Savitri and Kottarakkara shadow (chapter 27), "Every hoofprint has a name beneath it", "the land remembers longer than crowns do", and "Travancore has its own tools".
- **Bleeds.** One memory bleed, 6.4, sits behind Nagoji as the device does in Chapter 1: the Goa ledger, described so it stands alone, with no letters or numerals. The 8.5 bleed is imagined, not remembered, so it gets a different, desaturated treatment and shows the Nashik fields whole, with smoke only starting on the horizon; in the novel they never burn.
- **Callbacks.** The boots left at the verandah step (2.4) become the "path" image in 9.2 and return in the 11.3 inset and in 12.1. The storm clause in 11.4 calls back to Chapter 3.
- **Continuity.** Nagoji is in the chapter 7 to 16 commander sheet, with a clean-shaven chin, thick moustache and no facial scar, but with his sleeves down (the sheet shows them rolled) so that the arm is bared only in 6.2 and on page 12. He is barefoot from 2.4. The brand and the long white scar are both on his left forearm, positions fixed in the standing notes. Revathi wears no tali, because she is a widow until chapter 20. Padmini and Revathi, who have no reference sheets, carry ID tags in every panel.
- **Staging.** Nothing is touched on page 12; the near-touch in 12.4 carries the scene.
- **Page turns and imposition.** Turns are marked after pages 2, 9 and 11 only. Since the page 4 split (2026-10-04), pages 9 and 11 fall on versos when page 1 is a verso, so those two turns now open onto a spread; restoring them needs an imposition change (author decision). They land as written only if chapter 9 page 1 falls on a verso (left-hand page), which puts the Revathi reveal (3.1) and "Not from mine" (10.1) each behind a turn.
