## Chapter 13: The Eve of Colachel

Source: `book1_horse_servant/book2_chapter13_eve_of_colachel.md`, 4,407 words; 21 pages, 79 panels.

### Lettering and art notes

- Speech over a cutaway or a flashback, tagged "(voice-over)" or "(memory)", is lettered as a tailless voice-over box in that speaker's balloon style, never as an off-panel balloon with a tail. "(off)" means the speaker is just out of frame in the same place.
- Only panel text reaches the art prompt, so these chapter looks are repeated inside the panels that need them. Dhanaji: stockier and heavier than Nagoji, a short black beard, white Maratha pagdi turban, indigo quilted tunic, no sash, talwar; never the rust-red turban. Ponnan: bare-headed, short curly hair, heavy jaw, red shoulder cloth, short dark jacket, white mundu hitched, on a smaller Maravar pony. Kayal: Nagoji's bay mare, ochre red-brown coat, no white markings, sized between a Madurai charger and a Maravar pony. Nair soldiers: bare-chested, white mundu tied short, hair in a front topknot, no coats or uniforms, matchlocks. Night light: small brass oil lamps with open wicks, no glass chimneys. War-hall map: hand-drawn ink on cloth, lines only, no lettering, no place names, no compass rose; pebble and shell tokens and one small copper Dutch coin whose face is too small to read.
