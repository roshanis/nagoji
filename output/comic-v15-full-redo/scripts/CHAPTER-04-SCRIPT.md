## Adaptation notes

- **Page budget.** Six pages, 29 panels, on target. The vote on the beach (page 3) and the Travancore exchange (page 4) get a full page each because they are the chapter's drama: a stranger's life decided by a haggle, and the first time anyone offers him a future ("may be useful"). The walk inland and the village are compressed into the top of page 5.
- **Language convention.** The novel reports the Malayalam argument even though Nagoji "could not follow" it. The comic does the same with angle-bracket balloons from 3.1 onward, keeps his POV honest with the page 3 caption ("I did not need to"), marks the Fisherman's switch to Konkani with the one plain balloon at the end of 3.5, and uses the boy's untranslated balloon on page 6, where he catches only *kapitan*. The off-panel voices in 1.4 stay plain: they are the fragments that "began to separate" as he came to.
- **Cut: the first "sea gods had eaten enough" line.** The novel uses the idea twice; the comic keeps the later, sharper version over the wreck, where "No bodies" also quietly carries Keshavrao, lost in chapter 3.
- **Trimmed: the Wary Fisherman's objection** loses its Dutch clause; the Dutch arrive one balloon later from the Fisherman (3.4) and again in 4.2.
- **Cut: the carry inland in detail** (sand grit at the heels, the crow, the furtive pace). Kept the smell of earth and "these small things felt like gifts"; the untouched wreck survives as art in 5.1.
- **Cut: "The kapitan. A local headman, or something more?"** The question is raised by the art and answered by the final panel.
- **Cut: the kapitan's entrance line about "salt and foreign places".** Kept the horse-trader and market-square lines, which tie the chapter's end to his horseman's eye and to the slave ship.
- **No nudity, no gore.** The woman strips only the tunic; the dhoti stays. His ruined fingers are never shown directly; her face carries them (5.5).
- **Irons.** The novel leaves Nagoji shackled when he jumps in chapter 3 and never says how the irons came off. This script shows no irons from 1.1, only chafe rings at the ankles, and repeats "no irons" in every full-figure panel. The author may want a wordless bridge (for example a snapped cuff on the sand in 1.2); none is drawn here, to keep the model from putting irons back on him.
- **Brand placement (BIBLE CHANGE NEEDED BEFORE GENERATION).** The brand is on the inner side of the LEFT forearm, a hand's width above the wrist, drawn as raised ridges blurred past reading. That matches the bible's chapter 7 to 16 row ("left wrist or forearm"), the chapter 7, 8, 9, 10 and 17 scripts, and the novel's chapter 17 ("my left wrist, where the Portuguese brand still sat ... the ridged letters"). It departs from the novel's chapter 4 detail of the cloth tugged off his shoulder, which is the only place the book puts the mark near the shoulder. CONTINUITY.md's chapter 4 row still says "upper arm", and the pipeline copies that row into every chapter 4 prompt that shows Nagoji, so the row must be changed to the left inner forearm before generation. The chapter 5 script (1.1: "upper arm, same arm and placement as Chapter 4") needs the same change. Neither file was edited in this pass.
- **The kapitan's look.** CONTINUITY.md gives Ibrahim only his build, the LEFT scar and the chapter 6 coat. This script adds a white turban, a short grey-flecked beard, and a pale vest over a white mundu. Add these to his bible entry and carry them into chapter 5 (1.1) and chapter 6 (1.2), or his look will drift.

Each keeps his colour on his cloth and lungi (white cloth and red lungi; faded blue cloth, indigo lungi and headband; dark brown cloth, off-white lungi and net).
- **The Woman.** She is unnamed (the healer of chapter 5) and is drawn to be told apart from the chapter 8 estate matron: heavier-set, low grey knot, betel-stained lips, no gold, no stick, coarse undyed cotton with no fitted blouse. Do not give her any principal's look or reference sheet.
