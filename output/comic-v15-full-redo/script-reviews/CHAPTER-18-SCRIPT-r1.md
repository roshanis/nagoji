## Adaptation notes

- **Page count.** 10 pages against a target of 9. At Chapter 1's density, 2,651 words would need 12 or 13 pages. The middle of the chapter is a sustained verbal duel in one room, and each exchange lands on its own turn, so pages 2 to 7 are spent there. The rumour opening and the aftermath are compressed.
- **Opening rumour montage** reduced to two panels (market, temple). The backwaters and boatmen are cut. Yusuf keeps one caption because he returns in chapter 23.
- **Treaty clauses.** Only three core terms and the alliance clause are kept. Cut: timber access, protection of company factors, and Ramayyan's "only a pause for breath" (the Mannar/Mavelikkara line in 7.3 carries the same idea).
- **Cut: the pirate clause and joint-commission exchange** (Revathi's "Who decides who is a pirate?" and Ramayyan's "Interesting"). The novel never reads the piracy clause aloud, so the exchange would need a bridging term, and page 4 has no room. Revathi keeps her voice with the stronger question about the dead.
- **Cut: Ramayyan's summary of the offer** ("You offer recognition... You offer trade...") as it repeats the terms and Varma's replies.
- **Moved:** Padmini's "a pretty word for greed" now follows the envoy's "misunderstandings" directly, where it bites.
- **Varma's speech** trimmed to its load-bearing lines. Cut: the clause naming Velinadu and Padmini's line, and "safe anchorage"; kept "not sign away our children's choices" and "This army is not for hire", which Nagoji, a former hired sword, hears differently from anyone else in the room.
- **Lannoy and the envoy.** Cut the opening "You seem healthy" / "Men here do not waste tools" to keep 7.4 and 7.5 short; the betrayal, Amsterdam and kapitan lines stay verbatim. The chiefs' and priests' muttering after the session is background art in 7.4.
- **Verandah.** Revathi's closing speech is trimmed and split over 9.1 to 9.3. The "small, steady suns" image is art direction, not a caption.
- **Ibrahim.** Cut "They carry pepper and cloth and silver. They carry talk too" and "might one day threaten Maratha borders". Nagoji's "I felt the room tighten around those names" becomes the four-word caption in 10.2.
- **Continuity flag.** The novel says Nagoji has already seen Elayadathu marked HIGH in Ramayyan's register, but chapters 14 to 17 never show that scene. The 5.5 inset shows the leaf without implying a specific earlier moment. Check against chapter 21 (Ramayyan's Ledger) before drawing.
- **Wardrobe.** Lannoy follows the continuity bible (white mundu, cotton coat) merged with the novel's European shirt and cloth-wrapped hilt. Nagoji's silver chain appears from 2.1 onward and not in 1.4. Revathi wears no tali. The Dutch envoys are in sober civilian coats so they never read as blue-coated VOC soldiers.
- **Print.** Every placed frame for this chapter must reach at least 300 effective PPI at its printed size, per the continuity bible's print rule; upscale or regenerate anything below that before placement.
