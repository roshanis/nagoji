Examples: Nagoji's sheet on the parents (2.1, 2.2), on the Pune darbar (3.5), on Padmini alone (1.3, 4.3, 11.5) and on the dark sea (9.4); Ibrahim's look in 4.4; Keshavrao's look in the letter panel (10.3) and in the name panel (12.3).

- A 300 PPI print note was added, in line with the author's print decision. Every stacked panel is full width (369 pt, 5.125 in) and needs at least 1,538 px of visible width after the crop. A native 1,536 px strip reaches only 299.7 PPI, so each strip is generated wide and upscaled 2x with Real-ESRGAN before placement. The half-width 12.4 and 12.5 each need at least 765 px.
- The Chapter 18 callback for Ibrahim's wind line (4.4) was checked. That script keeps the line in its panel 10.3, and the notes now cite it.
