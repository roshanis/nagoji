## Adaptation notes

- **Exposition compressed so the night gets six pages.** The war hall runs about 40% of the novel chapter; here it takes three pages (6 to 8). The grove, Revathi and Dhanaji get pages 10 to 15, because the drama is there.
- **Reports (pages 1 and 2).** The inland chiefs are cut down to one caption. The Kottarakkara silence and Revathi's cold letter stay, because Kottarakkara pays off in ch14 and ch21, and the letter sets up her appearance in the grove. The name caption in 12.1 ("who had written that she would remain within her halls") is the one addition. It links the two scenes and invents nothing.
- **Padmini's day (page 3).** Her threat that grain-sellers would "eat dust for a month" is cut. Kept in novel order, so her sunset blessing closes the page and the caption "While she moved through her layers of responsibility, I moved through mine" takes the reader back to daylight on page 4.
- **Beach and courtyard.** Nagoji's speech to the riders is trimmed to its two key sentences. The detail that the Nairs trained under a European instructor is cut.
- **War hall economics (page 6).** Ramayyan's command at Kollam and against Desinganadu and the "rice bags and banners" passage are cut down to one caption. "The Java War" becomes "fires in the east". Van Imhoff's blockade, the chumkam explanation, and the move of the pepper trade inland to Kottar become Tharakan's line, Varma's "pepper has legs", and a wordless cutaway of bullock carts under Ramayyan's line.
- **The year before (page 7).** Compressed to one flashback page. Cut: van Gollenesse by name, the 22 Dutch dead and 17 wounded, the five Jesuits in the ship's cabin, Leslorant by name, the unfinished water tank and the arrack, and the lascars and ensigns. Kept: the November bombardment's "message written in salt and ash", the slave prices, the collapsing bastions, and Hackert leaving Rijtel with 250 men (all three names recur in ch14). Ramayyan's line about children kept by the soldiers is cut. The longboat at distance in 7.3 implies it without putting it in a balloon beside the image of children.
- **Coalition (page 8).** The Nedumangadu princess-regent's letter and Desinganadu halting a war for a festival become Varma's single line about Dutch coin.
- **"Two thousand men" cut.** In the novel Varma gives this as the Dutch strength, but it contradicts the 250-man garrison two pages earlier. Nagoji's "Against our thousands" goes with it, and his interrupted "If we charge them..." carries the beat. Varma's two thousand southern levies are shown as map tokens, not stated.
- **Dhanaji (pages 10 and 11).** "It feels different here. There, we rode for hills we knew" is folded into Nagoji's single line about the king and the gods. "That is enough for now" is cut to keep the balloon short.
- **Grove (pages 12 and 13).** Revathi's "Do you understand the significance of this" and "Songs will be written about men like you" are cut. Every other grove line is verbatim, including "He needs their permission to bleed on their earth", which Nagoji quotes back in ch16, and the serpent imagery that names him Ananthan in ch17.
- **Sleep (page 15).** The novel's three dream fragments are one split panel with no text, so they read as images, not narration. "Before dawn" is dropped from the last-fragment caption because 15.5 shows the dawn.
- Continuity held to `CONTINUITY.md`. Nagoji wears the ch7-16 commander look throughout, with a clean-shaven chin and a thick moustache. Revathi is still a widow here, so no tali. The Dutch are the blue coats. No gore; the Tengapattanam raid is shown only at a distance and as aftermath.
