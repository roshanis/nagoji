> NAGOJI: Then we will have walls ready for them.

> LANNOY: And guns. And horse. And men who know how to move on sand.

**20.5** Wide, the last light. The two men on the southern wall, the two Nair shadows a few paces behind them. Far below, a new unit marching past in neat order, muskets on their shoulders.

> CAPTION: Two foreigners, bound to a king who trusted us enough to let us build his teeth, and distrusted us enough never to look away for long.

> CAPTION: Travancore was changing.

> CAPTION (separate, weighted): So was I.

## Adaptation notes

- **Page budget.** 20 pages against a target of 19. Seven pages (3 to 9) go to Revathi, because the romance is the chapter's emotional core and the change in Nagoji ("a man who wanted one") drives later chapters. Megha's death gets two pages as the frame. The fortification, drill and levy exposition, about half the chapter's words, is held to five pages (10 to 15), and the Lannoy confession and the Udayagiri shots get the remainder.
- **Flashback.** The novel opens four monsoons on, at Megha's grave, then drops back to the weeks after Colachel. The script keeps that order and asks for a softened palette on pages 3 to 9.
- **Cut: Lannoy's second audience** ("I will serve. Under your conditions." / "We will see what kind."). Chapter 15 already dramatizes his acceptance and his first morning as a Travancore officer, so restaging it here would repeat a scene.
- **Cut: the Zeeland sergeant's speech.** The Company's unpaid wages and the weevils line come back in Lannoy's confession (16.5), so the comic gives them once, to the character who matters. The deserters are reduced to one caption and one image (11.1).
- **Cut: drill detail.** The friendly-fire exchange about the Low Countries, the thumb and recoil correction, and the lesson on aiming at belts. The two "Again" calls and the deep-blocks argument carry the partnership.
- **Cut: Ramayyan's "Learn to share walls" mediation and the king's silent drill inspections.** Both are charming, but there was no room. Ramayyan is carried by the pay caption and the shooting report.
- **Cut: Padmini's speech to her reluctant kinsman, and the paragraph on chiefs' retainers being folded into company units.** The parade and Revathi's critique carry the social change.
- **Cut: Lannoy's line about a captain in Amsterdam, his boyhood drawings of fortresses ("None of us drew the people who would live behind those walls"), and "the admission hung between us".** These were cut for balloon space on pages 14 and 17. The boyhood line is the strongest loss and can be restored at 17.3, in place of "My brothers have forgotten my face", if the page can take it.
- **Cut: "Always counting" / "It is what I know how to do", and the closing prophecy** of baffled Dutch, Portuguese and Mysorean officers ending in "That was enough for now". The chapter ends on "So was I", which is cleaner for a comic page.
- **Adjusted:** Padmini's reported warning becomes the balloon "Do not bleed on my courtyard." Varma's glance moves to the line "This army is not only for white men". The novel has it land "when he said Tamil", a word his speech never uses. Long speeches are trimmed, but the key phrases stay verbatim.
- **Staging choices:** the intimacy is implied through the flint and lamp, her unpinned hair, two shadows meeting on the wall and a burnt-out lamp at dawn. Afterward the two are framed at the shoulders under a cloth. There is no nudity. The Udayagiri shots are shown through a lifted strand of hair and chipped stone, and nobody is hit. Revathi's husband falls at a distance in the memory.
- **Continuity:** first grey at Nagoji's temples appears in present-day panels only, not in the flashback. Nagoji wears the blue Travancore drill coat only on parade (14.2). At Tiruvattar, Varma wears a plain white cloth with no crest, as the novel describes, and court dress elsewhere. The V13 temple priest face is used only for the Chief Priest at Tiruvattar (10.3). The Travancore blue-coat infantry keep dhotis, bare feet and the conch standard, so they do not read as VOC troops.
- **Print:** every placed frame must reach at least 300 effective PPI at its printed size (author instruction). Upscale 2x or regenerate any frame that falls below.
