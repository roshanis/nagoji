Adjustment: the envoy does not glance at Revathi, because the novel says he looked at no one.
2. **5.4, "Support those who feel aggrieved" (major).** APPLIED. It is the explicit threat to back a claimant house; 5.5 now opens on "Somewhere, someone the Dutch could use was waiting."
3. **9.1 to 9.3, Revathi's thesis and the order of her speech (major).** APPLIED. Restored "Few will talk of the quiet days..." and the novel's order ("I will remember", then "Good. Because one day..."). "Afterward" becomes "after Colachel" because the sentence before it is cut, and the stones caption moved to 9.4.

6. **8.4, "That is a small victory" (major).** APPLIED. Verbatim; without it, "Small victories add up" has nothing to answer.

8. **7.2 and 7.3, the envoy's request and the real-treaty clause (minor).** APPLIED. The request makes "We will draft our own version" a refusal, and "the one where you promise never to stand in our way again" is restored.
9. **3.4, "We underestimated the strength and resolve of Travancore" (minor).** APPLIED. Verbatim, and it is the Dutch bowing in words.
10. **10.4, "If he lies to me, another strand will tell me" (minor).** APPLIED. Without it, "I trust his web" reads as trust in Ibrahim. "When the first letter comes" gets its own inset beat.
11. **2.3, quotation marks on "Maratha" (minor).** APPLIED. The novel quotes it as the Dutch label for a man who is now Ananthan Pillai.
