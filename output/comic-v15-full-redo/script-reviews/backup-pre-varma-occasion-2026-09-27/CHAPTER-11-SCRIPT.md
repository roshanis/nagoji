## PAGE 20

Five panels. Dusk with Ramayyan. Why this coast needs other men's hands.

**20.1** Dusk in the emptying hall, the last chiefs drifting out. Ramayyan adding final lines to his leaves by lamplight, Nagoji seated on the mat beside him (commander sheet, thick moustache, clean-shaven chin).

## Adaptation notes

- **Page count.** 21 pages, 102 panels (two of them split tiers, 9.4 and 14.5) and one inset (11.4), against a target of 20. The extra page goes to the court scene (pages 8 to 14), which is where the chapter's drama lives: Batavia, Revathi's Portuguese, the rope, Kottarakkara, and "And our sand."
- **Opening exposition compressed.** The Dutch history on the coast (Kochi taken in 1663, raja played against raja, the two pepper hungers) is cut. What stays: the probing-squadron premise (1.1), so the reader does not wait for a battle that never comes; the Kollam campaign and the shark (1.5); and the "thick ink, thin whispers" motif, carried by Ibrahim on 3.4.

Ibrahim's "without losing too many shoes" and "Not close enough to risk your cannon" are cut, as is Ramayyan's reply about the sand drills; Nagoji's question about the drills is kept so that "And our sand" on 14.2 has a setup.
