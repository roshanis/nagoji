# V15 graphic novel against the 2e text: where they differ

Date: 2026-10-21. Author's choice: "Bring V15 up to the 2e text" (decision card, 2026-10-21).

Method: each 2e chapter in `manuscript/01_manuscript_2e_chapters.md` was compared sentence by sentence with the 1e chapter file in `book1_horse_servant/` that V15 was adapted from. Every changed 1e sentence was then searched for in the V15 lettering and panel descriptions (final builds per `V15-FINAL-BUILDS-2026-10-10.json`, 1,709 panels). Nothing has been changed yet. Anything proposed from this map goes through the review panel before you see it as ready.

## Summary

- 14 chapters have no 2e changes at all (8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 17, 19 to 23, 26, 27). Their V15 pages already match.
- Ch 3, 18, 25 and 28 changed only in sentences V15 never lettered. No V15 change needed.
- **Group A, cut kickers (lettering only):** 11 panels in ch 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 11 letter end-of-scene lines that 2e cut.
- **Group B, italics (lettering only):** 5 balloons need Portuguese words in italics.
- **Group C, De Lannoy deserts instead of surrendering (lettering and art):** this is the big one. 2e follows the settled plan (PLAN.md decision D2): De Lannoy crosses to Travancore at Kanyakumari eight days before Colachel falls. V15 still shows the 1e version, where he surrenders with the garrison and speaks for it. It touches ch 11, 14, 15, 16 and 24. Some of it is lettering only, but ch 14 page 12 and about a dozen ch 15 panels show him in the wrong place, and only new art can fix that.

## Group C: De Lannoy's desertion (2e) against his surrender (V15)

### Ch 11, Dutch on the Horizon
- **p1.2 to p2.3, new beat:** 2e opens "The first time I heard De Lannoy's name, it came not in a court, but from a boy on a watch tower," and the boy says: "They shout something. Hard to hear over the wind. Lannoy. Lannu. Something like that." V15 has the boy and the tall pale captain but no name. This needs one or two new balloons on p2, or a new panel if they don't fit. Lettering, maybe one panel of art.
- **p3.2:** NAGOJI: "The same tall officer on the quarterdeck. Pale hair, stiff back. He drills them hard." 2e: "...He calls him Lannoy, though the wind may have changed the name." Lettering.
- **p24.1:** see Group A.

### Ch 14, The Siege of Colachel
- **New beat, no V15 panels exist:** eight days before the end, the tall officer is gone from the Dutch ramparts. The watch boy calls down: "The pale one with the stick is gone." Ramayyan reads an ola: "Eustache Benoit de Lannoy, born in Arras. A captain..." Nagoji: "A deserter." Varma: "A pragmatist... Bring him to me after this is done." This is about one new page of art. A natural place is after p6 ("Yet they held"), before the 10 August page.
- **p12.1:** art shows survivors "with hands raised". 2e: they "fell back into the ruins of their stockade". Small art fix. The caption "They had not surrendered meekly." is not in 2e but doesn't contradict it.
- **p12.2 to p12.5, restaged:** V15 has De Lannoy walk out *from the Dutch lines*, introduce himself in Portuguese ("I am Eustachius De Lannoy. Captain of the Dutch East India Company. I speak for the garrison."), then trade lines with Ramayyan ("Because dead men cannot teach you..."). Nagoji's caption reads "It was the first time I heard those words from his own mouth." In 2e he comes *from our lines*, in his blue coat with a strip of Travancore cloth knotted across it, speaks to the Dutch in their own tongue, some of them spit, and a Dutch sergeant steps forward to parley. The Ramayyan exchange is gone (its "rather build than rot" moved to the ola). Page 12 needs new art and new lettering.
- **p11.5:** "hands going up" in the background. Optional small art fix, for the same reason as p12.1.

### Ch 15, Prisoners of a New King
- **p1.2:** art shows De Lannoy *inside the Dutch column*. In 2e he is on our side. Art. The captions still match 2e.
- **p2.4:** Ramayyan rides forward "alone". 2e: "with De Lannoy at his side". Art.
- **p2.5:** DONNADI: "We have terms!" / "You agreed to let us march to Kanyakumari with our arms!" 2e keeps "We have terms!", cuts the second line and adds: "Traitor. You negotiated this trap." Lettering, and possibly art if Donnadi should be looking at De Lannoy.
- **p3.1:** RAMAYYAN: "Terms change when kings have time to think." / "You burned our temples..." Both cut in 2e. The panel could go, or carry the next beat.
- **p3.2:** De Lannoy leans down to Donnadi's ear: "Joseph. Look around you. This is not a battle we can win." / "The alternative is worse than surrender." In 2e he calls across from our side: "I negotiated your lives. The alternative was to let you starve until the monsoon drowned what the siege did not kill. Take what is offered, Joseph." Art and lettering.
- **p4.3:** Donnadi looks at De Lannoy "at his shoulder". 2e: "standing with the enemy, already wearing their cloth." Art.
- **p5.3:** CAPTION: "Twenty-four European officers." 2e: twenty-three. Lettering.
- **p5.4:** De Lannoy among the prisoners; CAPTION: "De Lannoy looked at those walls as if he were already measuring them for improvement." 2e: "De Lannoy, who had crossed to us eleven days before, watched his former comrades march into captivity with an expression I could not read." Art and lettering.
- **Bandaged left hand (p1.2, 2.3, 3.2, 4.3, 5.4, 9.3, 11.1, 13.2, 16.1, 17.3, 18.1, and the continuity bible):** 2e drops the wound ("washed and shaved"). A deserter could still have a bandage, so this can stay. I'd leave it unless you want it gone.
- **p10.1:** DE LANNOY (off): "I am a Captain of the Dutch East India Company." 2e: "I was..." Lettering.
- **p12.1:** CAPTION: "To go from a prisoner in a dungeon to the commander of the army that had defeated him." 2e: "To go from a deserter under guard to the commander of the army that had defeated his old masters." Lettering.
- **p13.1:** NAGOJI: "...spear you on the beach." 2e: "...spear you at Kanyakumari." Lettering.
- p9.3 (brought in between guards) and p16.2 ("since I was seventeen", which fits an age of 25 after eight years' service) agree with 2e as they stand.

### Ch 16, Building a New Army
- **p18.3:** LANNOY: "A Catholic city in Flanders." 2e: Artois. Lettering.
- **p19.1:** LANNOY: "But seventeen years of watching the Company..." 2e: eight years. Lettering.
- **p20.2:** LANNOY: "I am forty-three." 2e: twenty-five. Lettering. The art is fine: his V15 character sheet is young, with pale blond hair.
- p18.1 ("Why did you cross? Before the battle ended...") already fits the desertion.

### Ch 24, Under De Lannoy's Standard
- **p9.2:** LANNOY: "When the fort burned at Colachel, I expected to die." 2e: "When I crossed the lines at Kanyakumari, I expected to die. I had seen what happens to European officers who walk into an Indian camp without a flag behind them." Lettering. The balloon gets longer.
- **p13.1:** LANNOY: "In Zeeland, where I was born, there is a saying..." 2e: "where I first took ship". Lettering.

## Scale

- Lettering only: about 35 balloons and captions on about 30 panels across 9 chapters. The V15 lettering pipeline can redo these from the scripts.
- New or redrawn art: ch 14 (one new page, page 12 restaged, plus two small fixes) and ch 15 (about 6 panels on pages 1 to 5). Possibly one panel in ch 11.
- Page count: ch 14 grows by about one page. That shifts the print folios and the page turns after it, so the print imposition and the digital edition both need rebuilding.
- The pipeline (Codex image generation, lettering, renders) and the page images are on your Mac. Running it means a session on your Mac. This container can't run it.
