### Notes for the author (2e text, not the lettering)

A. Twenty-three officers (item 19). The Note gives no officer count for the Colachel prisoners. The 1e figure of twenty-four apparently included De Lannoy; 2e subtracts him. Worth checking against Shungoonny Menon's list and the Company records in Mark de Lannoy (1997), since the Note already says the Company's records differ from Menon on where the prisoners went. Not a reason to hold the lettering.

B. Eight years of service (item 24). Neither the Note nor `kulaperumal.codex.md` gives when De Lannoy joined the VOC or reached Ceylon. "Eight years" (joining at about 17, around 1733) is plausible but unverified; check de Lannoy 1997. The REVIEW_BACKLOG N-04 suggestion was "nine years"; 2e chose eight, which matches age 25.

C. Birthday discrepancy in the project's own sources. `kulaperumal.codex.md` gives 29 December 1715; REVIEW_BACKLOG N-04 gives 30 December; the Note says only "December 1715". He is 25 at Kanyakumari either way, and still 25 in the ch 16 bastion scene if it falls before the end of December 1741. If ch 16 runs into 1742 he would be 26. Settle the exact day in the codex so later documents do not drift.

D. Zeeland (item 27). Plan 2B says "Ch 24's Zeeland becomes Arras"; 2e instead keeps Zeeland as the port where he "first took ship". This no longer contradicts the settled fact (born in Arras) and is plausible, since the Zeeland chamber recruited and sailed from Middelburg and Vlissingen, but where he embarked is not in the sources. Confirm against de Lannoy 1997 or treat it as invention; the author may want to note that 2e departed from the plan's literal wording here.
