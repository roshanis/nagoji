## Verdict

| Measure | Call |

| Recommended intensity | **Deep** (line-level re-voicing in the cellar scene, the Lannoy confession and the Udayagiri aftermath; rebuilt ending) |

The chapter has a real spine and a machine-made skin. The spine is the best practical writing in Part III. There is a horseman burying his own horse. There is the bandage banter, and "who will plant your pepper?". A Deccan rider reads Lannoy's star fort against the hill forts he grew up under, and the mason says "Stone likes to sit square." Nagoji counts deaths by column, and Lannoy says "But he pays me. On time. In my hand." None of that should be touched beyond a word or two.

The skin fails in three ways.

1. **Two dense zones.** In the cellar night (L105 to 153) and the ending (L427 to 439), nearly every paragraph closes on a correction ("not the coupling of poems... something rawer"), a mirrored pair ("She was careful... I was careful"), a vague-depth line ("Something had shifted") or a self-summary ("So was I.").
2. **Summary bridges between the montage threads.** These are L23 to 27, L159 to 163, L183, L249 to 253, L263 to 265, L289 to 297 and L311 to 317. There are about twenty one-line signpost paragraphs, and they work like section headings.
3. **Duplication left by the unbuilt revision.** The Company pay grievance is told three times (L169 to 173, L353, L373). Lannoy accepts service twice (Ch 15 L251, and here at L175 to 181). The ledger idea appears four times in twenty lines (L353, L361, L369, L371).

On top of that sit hard anachronisms: "friendly fire", "war machines that regenerate", "project our power", "navigated these shifts", "spent cartridges" and "conscription". It is not a 5 because the drill ground, the fort reading and the counting exchange are the book's real voice, and a reader meets them in every third paragraph.

Three continuity faults change what several rewrites say, so settle them first. They are the De Lannoy arc (L341, L351, L359, L369), the duplicate acceptance (L165, L175 to 181) and the horse history (L5, L15). See Chapter notes 1 to 3.

I defended 30 flags. Most are drill inventories, dialogue that carries plot or character, and the VOICE_BIBLE touchstone at L3 to 9. The pattern auditor's density is right about the two dense zones and too strict about the drill ground.
