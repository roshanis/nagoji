## Verdict

| Measure | Call |
|:-|:-|
| Severity | **4 of 5** |
| Recommended intensity | **Deep.** Line-level re-voicing from the valley fight to the end (L283 to 529). Medium (tics plus tightening) for L3 to 281. |
| Estimated word cut | **About 20%** at line level (5,094 to about 4,100 words). **About 35%** if the author also cuts the temple section (L415 to 529), which I recommend (see Chapter notes 1). |

This is really two chapters. The first 280 lines are a sound campaign story with authored exchanges: the headman's "roads tax", the raja on his stool ("I sit on mine"), the Colachel spear "in their gut", "Ramayyan's ink on your tongue", the stirrup drag, the "startled cough", and Ponnan keeping an eye on royal credit. The tells there are local. There are one-line verdict paragraphs, about a dozen "Not X. Y." corrections, an eye or voice tag on most speakers (eyes 18 times, voice tags 10), and a handful of anachronisms (tripwire, trigger guards, patrol patterns, torching).

From the valley fight at L283 the density climbs sharply. It uses stock berserker kit ("The world narrowed", "something dark in me", "The red receded") and the book's most recognisable reveal ("a flicker of something in her eyes. Not fear, exactly. Recognition."). The court scene is written in modern idiom ("vivid reading", "potential subjects", "got out of hand", "berserkers", "It better not"). Nearly every scene closes on an aphorism. Then L417 to 529 re-stages Ch 22's chaver attack on the heir, in the chapter's densest slop, behind textual seams: leading spaces on 54 lines, straight quotes and a capital "King". Those seams suggest a separately drafted insert.

Why 4 and not 5: until L415 the chapter's bones are human, and the other characters' voices (raja, headman, Padmini, Ponnan) are mostly right. Why deep: the fight, the court scene and the temple scene need re-voicing, not deletions alone. The chapter's earned close (the basin at L385, the cane not struck at L389, and the king's private "Let them fear the Tiger's claws" at L407) is currently buried under three aphorisms and a duplicate set piece.

Scanner counts, first edition: 13 correction constructions (3 not_but, 7 not_opener, 3 comma_not), 5 "as if" (ration 1), 4 "like a" (ration 2), 5 soft adverbs (ration 2 in total), and 99 one-sentence paragraphs. After the confirmed fixes: 1 "as if" (L95), 2 "like" similes (L57 revised, L317), 0 soft adverbs, and one dialogue "not X" (L161).
