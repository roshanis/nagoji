## Summary

- Items from earlier reviews (Table A): 99. RESOLVED 41, STILL PRESENT 55, UNCLEAR 3.
- New findings from the full read (Table B): 53. None of these were in any earlier review. STILL PRESENT 44, UNCLEAR 9.
- Front matter cross-check (Table C): 15. STILL PRESENT 13, UNCLEAR 2.

The earlier reviews mostly asked for trims and style changes, and about 40 percent of their items are now done. The bigger problems are continuity breaks that no review caught: how Dhanaji reached Travancore, a leftover "fled the Peshwa" backstory, the horses, De Lannoy's birthplace and age, Revathi as wife versus "might have loved", scenes that refer to events that have not happened yet, and a Colachel sequence whose dates and numbers disagree across chapters 11 to 15. These need fixing before or alongside the anti-slop pass, because some of the fixes touch the same sentences.

## Highest-priority open items

1. **Dhanaji's origin (N-01, FM-02).** Two lines say he shared the Goa dungeon and the landing; ch1 to ch4 show Nagoji alone. Readers will notice. Needs a short arrival beat plus two line edits.
2. **The "fled the Peshwa" backstory (N-02).** Chapter 12 and chapter 25 describe a different Nagoji, a fugitive from Pune. Three line edits fix it.
3. **The horses (BL-01, N-03, FM-15).** Kanka is a stallion shot at capture in ch1 and ch6, and a mare killed at Vasai in ch7 and the prequel. Kayal vanishes after ch7, and ch16 mentions a mount "lost in the storm". Pick one history and carry it through ch1, ch6, ch7, ch8, ch14, ch16 and the prequel.
4. **De Lannoy (CL-01, N-04, N-15).** Chapter 16 still has him "crossing" before the battle ended; ch24 says he was born in Zeeland (history and ch15/ch16 say Arras); he is 43 in 1742 (historically 26); and he accepts service twice.
5. **Revathi (N-05, N-06).** Chapter 28 calls his wife a woman he "might have loved in another life", and chapter 25's letter praises a letter he burned.
6. **Callbacks to events that have not happened yet (RV-01, N-07, N-08, N-09).** The ch19 chaver (ch22 event, and wrong killer), the ch18 ledger register (ch21), the ch20 "marches" (ch22) and the ch21 broom (ch28).
7. **The Colachel sequence (N-10 to N-14, F13-01).** Karl August sketches a fort that does not exist yet; ch12 says the Dutch have not landed after ch13 says they did; the garrison is both 250 and 2,000; the eve-of-battle council plans a siege months old; the Dutch surrender at ch14:145 and then march out armed.
8. **Duplicate scenes (N-16, N-17).** Uniforms introduced in ch16 and again as new in ch24; two chaver attacks on the heir in ch22 and ch23.
9. **History fixes that can be made with a word or two (CL-05, RV-02, N-26, N-28).** Thrippadidanam was in January, not the monsoon; "Nair Brigade" is an 1818 name; there was no reigning Nayak of Madurai after 1736, and Chanda Sahib held Madurai; "Padmanabhapuram" is used before the book renames Kalkulam.
