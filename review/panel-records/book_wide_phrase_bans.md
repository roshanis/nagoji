# Book-wide phrase bans (Roshan, 17 October 2026)

No "bubbles" (any form), and no smiles or expressions crossing or going across a face. Each hit below quotes the original and suggests a cut in the author's own words.

| Chapter | Original | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| Ch 14, The Siege of Colachel | "Then his eyes found De Lannoy on our side, a strip of Travancore cloth knotted across his stained blue coat, and something harder crossed his features. "Traitor. You negotiated this trap."" | "Then his eyes found De Lannoy on our side, a strip of Travancore cloth knotted across his stained blue coat. "Traitor. You negotiated this trap."" |
| Ch 24, Under De Lannoy's Standard | "You will want to tear it off and burn it.” A faint smile crossed his face. “I recommend against burning it." | "You will want to tear it off and burn it. I recommend against burning it." (one speech; the joke carries the smile) |
| Ch 24, Under De Lannoy's Standard | "despite what Ramayyan tells you about my concerns.” A ghost of a smile crossed her face. “My concerns are real." | Cut "A ghost of a smile crossed her face." and run the two speeches together. |
| Ch 25, Ramayyan's Test | "The dry palm cracked, the ink bubbling as it turned to black smoke." | "The dry palm cracked, and the ink turned to black smoke." |

Books 2 and 3 have about three more hits between them, to be picked up when those books are edited.
